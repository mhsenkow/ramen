## Backstories

**Hale.** Ran the wet-ring glass before the colony had names for weather. He
worries the condensers more than he admits. Meet him in steam and green.

**Casimir.** Believes the drum wants a skyline — works climbing toward the axis,
not sprawling. His plot reads as a small tower town before you hear his voice.

**Idris.** Digs until the spoil tells him where the rock ends. Alone on a ridge
by choice. His claim is a dark cone of heaps.

**Ren.** Shares a meadow watershed and will not yield it. Rivalry is trenches and
fear-edges, not blades. Help his drainage and the press eases.

**Jules.** Looks like he belongs at the reactor; works like a man afraid of dust.
He plants condensers in the dune sea and levels pads as if rain were a promise.

**Oren.** Orchards on the wet margin. Harvests what he planted years ago and
amends ash into the same rows. Gaps in the canopy are his handwriting.

**Sable.** Mid-slope benches, one man, no followers he will name. Level brush,
small spoil, nothing wasted. Hard to reach on purpose.

**Lark.** Walks other people's plots and remembers who helped whom. High social;
will cross half a claim to tell you what Hale muttered about the glass.
